What this simulation is

  • A simplified lumped model of the bench prototype, built from its design geometry: 300 × 300 × 2 mm plates at 46.5 mm pitch and 4° tilt, a 3 mm black floor, a 4 mm cover at 24°, and uninsulated 5 mm PMMA walls.
  • Air marches up through the six gaps (floor gap first) and exchanges heat and vapour with the film below and the plate above. Heat transfer coefficient h = 2.5 + 4.5·v W/m²K in the gaps. Mass transfer uses the Lewis analogy, and brine vapour pressure is lowered with salinity.
  • Sunlight: 90 % of it passes the cover. Each plate and film absorbs 6 % of what reaches it, and the black floor absorbs most of the rest. Radiation between neighbouring surfaces uses 5.5 W/m²K.
  • Water that condenses on the cover is assumed to run down the south wall onto P2.
  • Sensors carry their real resolution: DS18B20 in 0.0625 °C steps, SHT31 ±0.2 °C and ±2 %RH noise, and 1 g scales.

Use it to choose fan and feed settings, to rehearse the test and its logging, and to know what readings to expect. It does not replace the MATLAB model, which remains the reference for validating against the measured data.
